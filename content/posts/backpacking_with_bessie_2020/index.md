# A backpacking trip around Langdale with Bessie
**15th - 17th July 2020**


I thought it would be nice to go on a backpacking and wildcamping trip with Bessie. Unfortunately dogs don't know how to pace themselves so she was knackered each night and on the last night it was incredible windy and the sound of the tent flapping really freaked her out. I actually had to carry her the at the end of the last day.
